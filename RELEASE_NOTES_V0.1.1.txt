OPEN FRONTIER — V0.1.1 "HELLO, DISCORD"
===============================================

A hotfix, and the headliner is something entirely new: Open Frontier
now announces itself on Discord. Two proper bug fixes came out of
playtesting it, including one that made an entire campaign unwinnable-
ish. Sorry about that one.


DISCORD RICH PRESENCE
------------------------------------------------------------
Open Frontier now shows up on your Discord profile while you play. No
account linking, no "sign in with Discord" prompt, nothing to approve —
if Discord is open, your status updates. That's it.

The small icon next to your name is whatever you're currently in:
your ship, the station you docked at, the carrier you parked your
shuttle inside, a weapons platform you manned. Hover it and you get the
full story — class, variant and name (or the type and name of a
station). Every ship series and every station, structure and turret in
the game has its own icon, mapped from the actual class data, so
nothing ever falls back to a generic blob.

The two lines of text rotate through your actual situation, changing
every ten seconds:

- Your cargo manifest — a page at a time if you're hauling more than
  three types, so the whole hold gets seen
- Where you are — sector, scenario, security level, station count and
  how far you are from the frontier
- Your faction's treasury and fleet size
- How much of the universe you've charted
- Whatever the sector and the scenario have to say about themselves

And the moment something real happens, presence updates instantly:
swapping ships, docking, jumping, entering or leaving the main menu, or
renaming anything. The rotation restarts on the spot, so you're never
staring at stale information.

If you want presence to show even when Discord ISN'T running, there's
an authenticated mode in the spectator component — it asks you to
authorise the app once. Off by default, because nobody should have to
log in to see a spaceship.


"A WORMHOLE TOO FAR" COULDN'T SAVE. FIXED.
------------------------------------------------------------
If you played the A Wormhole Too Far campaign, you probably gave up on
saving around day one. Sorry! Three of that scenario's planets were
missing a component, and the save exporter walked straight into it and
threw — so the game refused to save, with a big red exception to
show for it.

The planets are fixed, and the save code no longer lets a single
malformed object take your whole save file down with it. One bad unit
now costs you one bad unit, not your playthrough.


THE CAMERA FIXES
------------------------------------------------------------
Two small ones that were driving me mad:

- The main menu's spectator blackout used to draw ON TOP of the menus
  and HUD, so every camera swap blacked out the interface along with
  the view. It now draws underneath, exactly where a cinematic fade
  belongs.
- Looking up and down with the camera in the universe was inverted
  compared to the main menu. Swiping up now looks up in both. (The HUD
  camera used to raise and lower itself, which is the opposite sign to
  "pitch up", and the conversion kept the wrong one.)


Other notable things
------------------------------------------------------------
- Rich Presence shows your sector's real security level (the same
  value the game itself uses for asteroid yields and abandoned ships)
  and your faction's real exploration progress.
- Text lines are trimmed to Discord's length limit automatically, and
  cards with nothing to show drop out of the rotation, so a fresh
  pilot cycles fewer facts than a veteran.
- Presence title reads "InterstellarPilot: Open Frontier", overriding
  whatever name the Discord application is registered under.
- No impact on save file formats — V0.1.1 saves load in V0.1.0 and
  vice versa.
